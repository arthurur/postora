# Postora

Postora is a social media scheduling product for the Brazilian market, derived from Postiz.

## Language

**Postora**:
The product offered to Brazilian creators, business owners, freelance social media managers, and agencies, with a Portuguese-language experience.
_Avoid_: Postiz when referring to this product.

**Postiz**:
The upstream project from which Postora is derived. Its public marketing website is a reference for the Postora marketing site.

**Marketing site**:
The public pages that introduce Postora to potential customers before they enter the app.

**App**:
The authenticated Postora product where users schedule posts, view their calendar and analytics, manage teams, and organize media.

**Free trial**:
Postora's seven-day period for trying the product without a subscription charge. It is the offer referenced by "Começar por R$0".

**Channel**:
A connected social account, profile, or page in Postora. Plan limits count these connections, so several accounts on the same network use several channel slots.
_Avoid_: Social network when referring to a single connection.

**Supported social network**:
A publishing platform Postora offers to its customers. The initial marketing scope covers X, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, and Threads.
